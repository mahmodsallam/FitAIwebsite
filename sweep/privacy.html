Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

Summary: Sweep works entirely on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, we do not run servers that hold your data, and there is no account to create. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. The only service the App talks to is RevenueCat, which manages Sweep Pro subscriptions together with Apple.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Sweep ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the Sweep mobile application ("App"). Sweep helps you clean up your photo library by swiping through photos and videos to keep or delete them.

2. Information the App Accesses on Your Device

2.1 Photo Library

With your permission, Sweep reads your photo library (photos, videos, their dates, sizes and thumbnails) so it can show them to you, sort them by month, find screenshots, large videos and duplicate or similar photos, and show you memories from the same day in previous years. All of this analysis runs on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded or sent to us or to any third party.

When you confirm a deletion, Sweep asks iOS to delete the selected items. iOS shows its own confirmation, and deleted items go to your Recently Deleted album, where they stay for 30 days unless you remove them sooner. You can choose full or limited access, and change it any time in iOS Settings.

2.2 Decisions and Statistics

Your keep/delete decisions, daily swipe count, streaks, and the space you have freed are stored locally on your device. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the App removes this data.

2.3 Notifications

With your permission, Sweep schedules an optional daily reminder as a local notification at a time you choose. It is generated on your device. You can turn it off in the App's settings or in iOS Settings.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not collect or upload your photos, videos or their metadata.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage Sweep Pro purchases and to unlock Pro features. RevenueCat may collect limited information necessary to process and manage your purchase, including:

  • A device or app-specific identifier
  • Transaction identifiers and purchase history within the App
  • Subscription status and renewal information

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email or payment details, and it does not receive your photos. All payment processing is handled by Apple. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4.2 Apple

Downloads and in-app purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you share the App's savings summary, the iOS share sheet is handled by Apple and the app you choose.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

6. Data Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by iOS data protection. Because we do not operate servers that hold your personal data, there is no central database of user data that could be breached. Communication with RevenueCat uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Because your data lives on your device, you are in direct control of it:

  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App.
  • Revoke photo library or notification access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • For purchase data held by Apple or RevenueCat, refer to their privacy policies to exercise any rights that apply to you (including rights under GDPR and CCPA).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
