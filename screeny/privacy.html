Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Summary: Screeny keeps your data on your device. We do not operate servers, do not require an account, do not run analytics or advertising, and do not sell your data. The apps you choose to block are selected through Apple's Screen Time system and are never visible to us. Subscriptions are managed by RevenueCat and Apple.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Screeny ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the Screeny mobile application ("App"). Screeny helps you stay focused by blocking distracting apps, websites, and categories during focus sessions and asking you to solve a math problem before you can unlock them.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No accounts: The App does not require registration, sign-in, or any form of user account.
  • No servers of our own: We do not operate servers that store your personal data.
  • No analytics or tracking: We do not use analytics tools to track how you use the App, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No advertising: The App contains no third-party advertising.
  • No data selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to any third party.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data on your device only:

  • Your focus sessions, durations, streaks, and statistics
  • Your preferences and settings (for example math difficulty, strict mode, and daily reminder)
  • Your onboarding answers, such as your goal, age range, and estimated daily phone use, used only to personalize the App
  • Your selection of apps, websites, and categories to block, in the opaque form provided by Apple (see Section 4)

You can delete all of this data at any time by uninstalling the App.

4. Screen Time Access (Family Controls)

Screeny uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity) to block the apps you choose. With your permission:

  • You choose what to block in an Apple-provided picker. Apple returns your selection to the App as opaque tokens, so the App does not learn the names of your apps or your browsing history.
  • Your selection is shared with the App's shield extensions through a private App Group container on your device, so the blocking screen can appear.
  • This information is never sent to us or to any server.

You can revoke Screen Time access at any time in iOS Settings; blocking will then stop working.

5. Notifications

With your permission, the App can send a local daily reminder and session notifications. These are generated on your device. You can disable them at any time in iOS Settings or in the App's settings.

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage Screeny Pro purchases (a yearly subscription with a free trial, and a one-time lifetime purchase). RevenueCat may collect limited information necessary to process and manage your purchase, including:

  • An app-specific identifier
  • Transaction identifiers and purchase history within the App
  • Subscription status and renewal information

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment details. All payment processing is handled by Apple. You can review RevenueCat's privacy practices at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6.2 Apple App Store and Screen Time

When you download the App or make in-app purchases, and when you use Screen Time features, Apple may process information as described in their privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through this App, please contact us and we will assist where possible.

8. Data Security

Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (for example iOS data protection). Since we do not operate servers holding your personal data, there is no central database of user data that could be breached.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Because your data lives on your device, you are in direct control of it:

  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App.
  • Revoke Screen Time and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
  • For data held by third parties (Apple, RevenueCat), refer to their privacy policies to exercise applicable rights, including rights under GDPR and CCPA where they apply.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy on this page. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
