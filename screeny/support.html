Common questions

Blocking does not work

Open iOS Settings, then Screen Time, and make sure Screen Time is turned on. Then reopen Screeny and allow Screen Time access when asked.

How do I change the blocked apps?

Open the Settings tab in Screeny and choose Blocked apps.

Manage or cancel Screeny Pro

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also restore purchases from Screeny's Settings tab.
