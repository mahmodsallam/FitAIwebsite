Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2025

Your privacy is important to us. GlowMax does not sell your personal data. Face photos are processed solely to generate your analysis and are never stored on our servers.

1. Who We Are

GlowMax ("we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application that uses AI to provide facial analysis, personalized skincare routines, and improvement plans. By using GlowMax, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Contact: support@fitai-app.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Photos and Images

When you use the scan feature, your photo is sent to our AI analysis service. Photos are transmitted securely, used only to generate your analysis results, and are not stored after processing is complete.

2.2 Onboarding Information

During onboarding, we collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Age and gender
  • Skin type and skin concerns
  • Facial concerns and goals
  • Self-assessment responses (e.g., weakest feature, motivation)

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to personalise your analysis, routines, and tips.

2.3 Scan Results and Progress Data

Your scan scores, routines, and progress history are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. We do not sync this data to external servers.

2.4 Purchase Information

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. We do not receive or store your payment details. RevenueCat may collect anonymous usage and purchase data subject to their own Privacy Policy.

2.5 Usage Data

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage information (e.g., which features are used most often) to improve the app. This data cannot be used to identify you.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Generate your AI facial analysis, scores, and personalised routine
  • Personalise tips and recommendations based on your profile
  • Track your progress over time (stored locally)
  • Manage your subscription and entitlements
  • Improve app features and user experience
  • Respond to support requests

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with the following trusted third parties to operate the service:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — Your photo and profile context are sent to Anthropic's API via our secure proxy to generate analysis results. Anthropic processes this data per their Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — Used to manage subscription status. Subject to their Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store — Handles all payment processing.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Retention

Your profile and scan history are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. Photos sent for analysis are not retained by us after the analysis is returned.

6. Children's Privacy

GlowMax is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data. All communication between the app and our services uses HTTPS encryption. Photos are transmitted securely and not retained on our servers.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of non-essential data collection

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@fitai-app.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact:
