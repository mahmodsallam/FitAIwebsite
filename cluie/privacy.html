Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Summary: Cluie is an interactive mystery/detective story game. The "phones," messages, and photos you browse inside the app are fictional story content — we do not access any real third party's actual phone, messages, or accounts. This policy covers the real data we collect about you as a player.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Cluie ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the Cluie mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. What Cluie Is

Cluie lets you play through fictional "cases" by browsing a fictional character's simulated phone — their messages, photos, and notes — to piece together a story. Every character, message, and photo shown inside a case is fictional story content created for entertainment. The App does not access, request, or display any real person's actual phone, messages, contacts, or accounts.

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of data automatically when you use the App:

  • Device and install identifiers: An anonymous identifier generated on install, used to recognize your device across sessions and secured on-device via iOS Keychain.
  • Usage and analytics data: App interactions such as screens viewed, cases played, and in-app events, used to understand engagement and improve the App.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: Crash logs and performance data, used to find and fix bugs.
  • Purchase data: Subscription and purchase status, managed through Apple's App Store and our subscription provider.
  • Local app data: Your progress, answers, and settings, stored locally on your device.

We do not require an account to use the App, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact list.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services, each of which may collect certain data as described below. We do not control their data practices and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

4.1 Firebase (Google)

We use Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and Firebase Storage to deliver app content (such as case images). Firebase may collect device information and crash diagnostics. Review Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.2 Analytics (Amplitude, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer, Meta)

We use Amplitude and Mixpanel for product analytics, and AppsFlyer to understand how users discover the App. We also use Meta's (Facebook) App Events SDK for aggregated app event reporting for our own marketing measurement. These services may collect device identifiers, app usage events, and general device information (device type, OS version, coarse region).

  • Amplitude: https://amplitude.com/privacy
  • Mixpanel: https://mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • AppsFlyer: https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • Meta: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

4.3 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect a device or app-specific identifier, transaction identifiers, and subscription status. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment details — all payment processing is handled by Apple. Review RevenueCat's privacy practices at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

4.4 Google Mobile Ads (Advertising)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve rewarded video advertisements. We request non-personalized ads only, and the App does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Google's ad SDK may still collect general device information (device type, OS version) and ad interaction data needed to serve and measure non-personalized ads. Review Google's privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.5 Apple App Store

When you download the App or make in-app purchases, Apple may collect information as described in their privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

5. Advertising and Tracking

Cluie does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We serve non-personalized ads only and do not use IDFA or any cross-app tracking identifier.

6. Data Retention

We retain analytics and diagnostic data for as long as reasonably necessary to support the purposes above, typically no longer than 24 months, after which it is deleted or fully anonymized.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this App, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data Security

We do not operate backend servers that store personal profile data. Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (e.g., iOS data protection and Keychain).

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export the data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion; we do not sell personal information.

To remove all locally stored App data, simply uninstall the App from your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy within the App or on our website. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
