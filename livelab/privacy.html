Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 30, 2026

Summary: LiveLab does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data stays on your device. We do not sell your data. Third-party SDKs (RevenueCat and Google Mobile Ads) have their own data practices, which are described below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how LiveLab ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the LiveLab mobile application ("App"). LiveLab is an AI-powered practice environment designed to help aspiring content creators build live streaming skills in a safe, private setting.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

LiveLab is built with a privacy-first approach. We want to be transparent about what we do not do:

  • No personal accounts: The App does not require registration, sign-in, or any form of user account.
  • No data transmitted to our servers: We do not operate any backend servers. No information from your use of the App is sent to us.
  • No video or audio recording: Camera and microphone access is used exclusively on-device to simulate the practice session experience. No footage or audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • No location data: We do not access or track your location.
  • No contact or calendar access: We do not access your contacts, calendar, or other personal data on your device.
  • No behavioral analytics: We do not use analytics tools to track how you use the App.
  • No data selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to any third party.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device via the App and includes:

  • Your display name and username (set by you in your profile)
  • Your profile photo (stored locally on-device)
  • App preferences and settings
  • Practice session history and progress
  • Simulated follower, fan, and friend counts

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device.

4. Camera and Microphone

LiveLab requests permission to access your device's camera and microphone. These permissions are used solely to display the practice session on your screen and to enable on-device AI features. Specifically:

  • Camera input is displayed locally on your screen to simulate the appearance of going live.
  • Microphone input may be processed locally by Apple's on-device machine learning to generate contextual simulated audience responses during practice.
  • No video, audio, screenshots, or derived data are ever recorded, saved to storage, or transmitted over a network by the App.
  • All AI processing of camera and microphone input occurs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device machine learning frameworks.

5. On-Device AI

The App uses on-device AI to generate simulated audience interactions — comments, reactions, and engagement — based on what the camera sees and what the microphone hears during your practice session. This processing:

  • Happens entirely on your device.
  • Does not send any data to Apple's servers or our servers.
  • Is governed by Apple's privacy practices for on-device machine learning.

For more information, refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Services

The App integrates two third-party SDKs that may collect certain data as described below. We do not control their data practices and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

6.1 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect limited information necessary to process and manage your subscription, including:

  • A device or app-specific identifier
  • Transaction identifiers and purchase history within the App
  • Subscription status and renewal information

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment details. All payment processing is handled by Apple. Review RevenueCat's privacy practices at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

6.2 Google Mobile Ads SDK (Advertising)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve rewarded video advertisements. Google's SDK may collect certain data to serve ads, which may include:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, subject to your device's tracking permissions)
  • General device information (device type, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., whether an ad was viewed or completed)
  • Coarse location or IP-based region (for ad targeting)

Google's data collection is governed by their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

On iOS, Google's ad SDK respects the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If you have not granted tracking permission, personalized advertising will be limited or disabled.

6.3 Apple App Store

When you download the App or make in-app purchases, Apple may collect information as described in their privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

7. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

In accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, the App may request permission to track your activity across other apps and websites for advertising purposes (used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK). You can:

  • Choose to allow or deny this permission when prompted.
  • Change your preference at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Denying tracking permission will not affect your ability to use the App's core practice features.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the App itself does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no personal data at risk. However, due to advertising content, we recommend parental supervision for users under 13.

9. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on external servers, there is no personal data held by us that could be at risk of a data breach. Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (e.g., iOS data protection).

10. Your Privacy Rights

Since we do not collect, store, or process any personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. For data held by third-party SDKs (RevenueCat or Google), please refer to their respective privacy policies and contact them directly to exercise any applicable rights.

To delete all locally stored App data, simply uninstall the App from your device.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy within the App or on our website. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
