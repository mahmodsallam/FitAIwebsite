Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 9, 2026

Summary: SunKiss stores your data on your device. We do not operate our own servers, do not require an account, and do not sell your data. Your location is used only to fetch local UV and weather data from Apple WeatherKit. Skin-scan photos are analyzed by Anthropic's Claude AI and are not used to train AI models; the photos themselves are kept only on your device. Subscriptions are managed by RevenueCat and Apple.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how SunKiss ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the SunKiss mobile application ("App"). SunKiss provides real-time UV index information, personalized sun-safety guidance, tanning routines, and sun-exposure tracking.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal accounts: The App does not require registration, sign-in, or any form of user account.
  • No backend servers of our own: We do not operate servers that store your personal data.
  • No behavioral analytics: We do not use analytics tools to track how you use the App.
  • No advertising: The App contains no third-party advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No data selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to any third party.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device. It never leaves your device via the App, except where explicitly described in this policy:

  • Your skin profile (skin type, tone, tanning preferences, goals)
  • Skin-scan photos and their analysis results
  • Tanning sessions, sun-exposure history, and streaks
  • App preferences and settings (units, notifications, reminders)
  • Saved locations for weather lookups

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App.

4. Location Data

With your permission, the App accesses your device's location to retrieve the UV index and weather forecast for where you are. Specifically:

  • Your coordinates are sent to Apple WeatherKit to fetch local weather and UV data. This is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Your most recent coordinates are shared with the App's home-screen widget through a private App Group container on your device, so the widget can show UV data for your location.
  • We do not store your location history, and your location is never sent to servers operated by us.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings; you can still use the App by searching for a city manually.

5. Skin-Scan Photos and AI Analysis

Transparency note: When you use the optional skin-scan feature, the photo you take or select is transmitted securely to Anthropic's Claude AI service for analysis. This is the only case where a photo leaves your device through the App.

  • The photo is used solely to estimate skin tone, tan level, and related skin metrics that personalize your sun-safety plan.
  • Analysis requests are sent over an encrypted connection to the Anthropic API.
  • Per Anthropic's API data policy, API inputs are not used to train Anthropic's models. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • The photo itself and the analysis results are stored only on your device. We do not keep copies.
  • The skin scan is optional — you can always set your skin type manually instead.

6. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App can write your sun-exposure data to Apple Health, including estimated vitamin D intake, time in daylight, and UV exposure. In accordance with Apple's requirements:

  • HealthKit data is used only to provide the App's health-related features.
  • We never use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing, and we never disclose it to third parties.
  • You control this permission entirely and can revoke it at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

7. Notifications

With your permission, the App sends local notifications such as UV threshold alerts, burn-time warnings, morning forecasts, and reminders. These are generated on your device. You can disable them at any time in iOS Settings or in the App's settings.

8. Third-Party Services

8.1 Apple WeatherKit

UV index, weather conditions, and forecasts are provided by Apple WeatherKit. Location coordinates are shared with Apple to retrieve this data, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

8.2 Anthropic Claude (AI Skin Analysis)

Optional skin-scan photos are analyzed by Anthropic's Claude AI as described in Section 5. See https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

8.3 RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

We use RevenueCat to manage the SunKiss Pro subscription. RevenueCat may collect limited information necessary to process and manage your subscription, including:

  • A device or app-specific identifier
  • Transaction identifiers and purchase history within the App
  • Subscription status and renewal information

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment details. All payment processing is handled by Apple. You can review RevenueCat's privacy practices at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

8.4 Apple App Store

When you download the App or make in-app purchases, Apple may collect information as described in their privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/privacy/

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through this App, please contact us and we will assist where possible.

10. Data Security

Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (e.g., iOS data protection). Photo analysis requests to the Anthropic API are transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Since we do not operate servers holding your personal data, there is no central database of user data that could be breached.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Because your data lives on your device, you are in direct control of it:

  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App.
  • Revoke location, notification, or Health permissions at any time in iOS Settings.
  • For data held by third parties (Apple, Anthropic, RevenueCat), refer to their privacy policies to exercise applicable rights (including rights under GDPR and CCPA where they apply).

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy on this page. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
