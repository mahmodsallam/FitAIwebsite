Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 19, 2026
The short version: Istiqama does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your information stays on your device — always.

Introduction

Istiqama is a free iOS app designed to help Muslims stay consistent with their daily prayers. It blocks distracting apps during prayer times, lets you read and listen to the Quran, and helps you track your prayer completions.

Privacy is central to how this app was built. We believe an app that helps you focus on worship should never be in the business of collecting data. This policy explains clearly what information Istiqama uses, why it uses it, and what it absolutely does not do.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Here is a full picture of what Istiqama accesses and why:

  • Location (optional): Istiqama asks for your location permission solely to calculate accurate prayer times for your local area. Your location is used only on your device, never stored, never sent to any server, and never shared with anyone. If you deny the permission, the app lets you set your location manually.
  • No accounts or sign-ups: You do not need to create an account or provide any personal details to use Istiqama. There is no registration, no login, no profile.
  • No analytics or tracking: Istiqama does not include any analytics SDKs, crash-reporting libraries, or ad-tracking frameworks. We have no way to see how you use the app — and that is intentional.

How We Use Your Information

The only "information" Istiqama works with is your location — and it is used for one thing only: calculating your local prayer times. This calculation happens entirely on your device.

We do not transmit this data anywhere. We do not store it. We do not analyse it. Once you close the app, nothing about your location or behavior is retained by us.

Third-Party Services

Istiqama integrates with two Apple-provided frameworks. Neither gives us access to your personal data:

  • Apple Screen Time / Family Controls: Istiqama uses Apple's Screen Time API to block distracting apps during prayer times. This feature runs entirely on your device and is managed by Apple's own framework. At no point can the developer see which apps you use, how long you use them, or any other Screen Time data.
  • Apple StoreKit (optional tip/donation): Istiqama is free. If you choose to leave an optional sadaqah (tip), the purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details, no billing information, and no personally identifiable information from that transaction. Apple's privacy policy governs how they handle payment data.

Istiqama does not show any advertisements and does not share any data with third parties.

Cookies

Istiqama is a native iOS app and does not use cookies.

Children's Privacy

Istiqama does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because we collect no data at all, the app is safe for users of all ages. If you are a parent or guardian with concerns, please feel free to reach out.

Your Rights (GDPR & Privacy Laws)

Because Istiqama collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. However, if you are based in the EU or another region with data protection laws and have any questions about your rights, you are always welcome to contact us.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, the new version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date. We will never change our practices in a direction that weakens your privacy without clearly communicating it.

We encourage you to revisit this page occasionally, though we expect changes to be rare.
